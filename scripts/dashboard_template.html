Data: Meridian Supply Chain Dataset (Kaggle, nadhirmazariboufares/supply-chain-dataset, MIT licence). This is a synthetic dataset; results describe a fictional company. Freight cost, on-time and damage flags are from the data; late penalties, damage write-offs and Air re-shipment costs are modelled from the assumptions above (defaults: 2% of revenue, 25% of units, CARR-3 Air rate). On-time % uses the source on_time_flag, which is inconsistent with the delivery dates in about half of shipments (see limitations.md).
